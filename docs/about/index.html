About & method

About Daily Logic Garden

Daily Logic Garden publishes five daily logic puzzles with three levels each, plus guides on rules and solving technique. It is free, needs no account, and keeps your progress on your own device.

Who publishes the site

The site is published by AdStern, an advertising agency in Vienna, Austria (details in the imprint). It is financed by advertising on puzzle and guide pages. No game publisher pays for mentions, and the guides contain no affiliate links. The site was rebuilt from scratch in November 2026; the previous version offered a small set of rotating nonogram boards, which are no longer published here.

How the puzzles are made

Each board is generated in your browser. The date in UTC, the puzzle type and the level form a seed for a deterministic random generator, so every device produces the same board on the same day. The generator proposes a layout – flower beds grown around a hidden solution, trees beside hidden tents, a full sun-and-shade grid, a random path through every square, a random assignment of gardeners – and then removes information (givens, stones, clues) as long as the puzzle stays solvable at its level.

How every board is checked

  • Exactly one solution. An exhaustive search, written separately from the logic solver, must find exactly one solution. (For 10 × 10 Sun & Shade boards the browser uses a faster exhaustive search with line propagation; the automated tests additionally run the plain row-by-row search.)
  • No guessing. A solver that only uses a fixed list of human techniques must finish the board.
  • Honest level. The solver must need its level’s technique, and must fail with the techniques of the level below.

Automated tests run these checks for 60 consecutive days for every puzzle and level, and they verify that following only the hints solves the board and that every hint agrees with the solution. The technique lists are described in the guides, for example Star Battle strategy.

How the guides are written and checked

Guides are written for this site. Numbers about our puzzles (board sizes, average steps, how often a technique was needed) come from running our generator on the 60 daily seeds from November 2 to December 31, 2026. Mathematical counts, such as the 34 valid rows of eight in a binary puzzle, were computed by our own code. Example boards in the figures are produced by the real generator when the site is built. Each page shows its review date; it changes when a page is checked again or corrected.

Limits

The difficulty levels reflect our solver’s technique order; a human may find another route. The daily switch depends on your device clock. Progress stored in the browser is lost if you clear site data, unless you exported it. We describe genres by their common names only so people can find the rules; we are not affiliated with any other puzzle publisher or game.

Corrections

Confirmed errors in puzzles, hints or guides are fixed and listed here with their date. No corrections have been necessary since the launch on November 2, 2026.

Your data

Puzzle progress and your garden stay in your browser’s local storage. Details on advertising and analytics are in the privacy policy.
