Contact

Daily Logic Garden is published by AdStern in Vienna, Austria. You can write to office@adstern.com; the postal address is in the imprint.

Reporting a puzzle problem

If a board seems to have two solutions, a hint seems wrong, or something does not work on your device, please tell us the puzzle, the level and the date shown above the board, and if possible what you saw. Confirmed problems are fixed and noted on the about page.

Ideas and feedback

We read every message about the puzzles. Suggestions for clearer hint wording, accessibility problems – for example a pattern that is hard to tell apart on your screen – and ideas for the guides are especially welcome. Please understand that we cannot answer every message individually, and that we do not publish user-submitted puzzles.

Schools and clubs

Teachers and puzzle clubs may print our sheets for their groups free of charge; please keep the footer line on each page. If you plan a larger event and want to use our boards, a short note in advance is appreciated.

What we cannot do

We cannot restore a garden that was deleted with your browser data, because we never receive it. We do not accept sponsored posts or paid links.

How we handle data is described in the privacy policy.
