Printable logic puzzles: weekly garden packs

Choose a week, a level and the puzzle types you like, and print them one per page – clear black-and-white boards with the rules at the top. Ideal for a quiet morning, a classroom warm-up or a long train ride.

Make a pack

Puzzles

The pack covers up to seven days, ending today at the latest.

How to make a pack

Pick the first day of your week – any day in the last 30 days – then the level and the puzzle types. Press Create and print the pack. The page grows the boards for up to seven consecutive days, one puzzle per page, and opens your browser’s print dialog. To keep a file instead of paper, choose “Save as PDF” as the printer; most desktop and phone browsers offer that option.

Each page carries the puzzle’s name, level and date, the rules in two or three sentences, a large board and the line “one solution, no guessing needed”. Because the boards come from the same seeds as the daily puzzles, the pack for a given week is exactly what was played online that week, so you can check a finished sheet by opening the same date in the archive and asking for hints.

All sheets are black on white, with no gray backgrounds that waste ink. The beds of Flower Beds are told apart by texture – dots, stripes in two directions, cross-hatching, rings, a fine grid – and every bed’s first square carries the first two letters of its name, so even a faint printer leaves the puzzle solvable.

A full pack with all five puzzle types for seven days is 35 pages. If that is more than you want, pick two or three types; the most popular combination for a coffee-table week is Flower Beds, Tents & Trees and Sun & Shade.

Who printable puzzles are good for

Paper puzzles have advantages that a screen can’t match. There is no device to charge, nothing beeps, and you can annotate freely: small dots in Flower Beds for squares you’re unsure about, pencil arrows in Garden Path, notes in the margins of a logic grid. Many people simply find it more relaxing to solve with a pencil.

  • Families and classrooms. Sprout boards are a gentle introduction to deductive reasoning. The rules fit in a few lines, and every step can be explained with “because”.
  • Older solvers. The printed boards fill most of the page width, so every square is large. Print at 100% on letter or A4 paper; our guide for seniors has more tips on sizes and pace.
  • Commuters and travelers. A week of puzzles weighs a few sheets of paper and needs no signal.

If you run a club or a class, you are welcome to print packs for your group. Please keep the footer line with our address on the sheets, so others can find the online versions and the hints.

Printing tips

For the cleanest result, set the scale to 100% (or “actual size”) and turn off headers and footers in the print dialog, so the browser does not add its own URL and page numbers on top of ours. Portrait orientation suits every sheet. If your printer offers a draft mode, the boards remain legible because they use solid black lines rather than gray tones.

You can also print a single puzzle from its own page with the Print button below the board. That prints the board you have open – for example yesterday’s Grove Tents from the archive – on one sheet.

Solutions are not printed. That is on purpose: every board can be solved by logic alone, and if you get stuck, opening the same date online and pressing “Why?” gives you the next step with its reason rather than the whole answer.

Questions and answers

Is the pack really free?

Yes. The pack is created in your browser from the daily seeds; there is no sign-up and nothing to buy.

Why can I only pick days from the last month?

The packs use the same dates as the online archive, which covers the last 30 days, so you can always check a sheet against the online version.

Can I get the solutions?

Solutions are not printed. Open the same date in the archive and use the hints to check or finish a sheet step by step.

Which paper size should I use?

Both US letter and A4 work. Print at 100% scale in portrait orientation.

Can I print a pack from my phone?

Yes, if your phone can print or save to PDF. The pack appears in the system print dialog just like on a computer.
