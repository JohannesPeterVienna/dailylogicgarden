Privacy policy

This policy explains what data is processed when you visit dailylogicgarden.com, why, on which legal basis, and which rights you have.

Controller

AdStern, Seilerstätte 8, 1010 Vienna, Austria, office@adstern.com. More details are in the imprint.

Hosting on GitHub Pages

The site is a collection of static files served by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. To deliver a page, GitHub necessarily receives technical data from your browser, such as your IP address, the requested address, date and time, browser type and the referring page, and may store it in server logs for a limited time for security purposes. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in delivering a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Data transfers to the USA are based on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Puzzle progress in your browser

Your moves, hint counts, times, chosen level, preferences and your garden are saved in your browser’s local storage on your device so you can continue later. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. You can delete it by clearing this site’s data in your browser; the export file on the “My garden” page is created on your device only. Storing this data is necessary to provide the puzzle function you request (§ 165(3) Austrian Telecommunications Act 2021).

Advertising by Google AdSense

Puzzle and guide pages show ads delivered by Google AdSense, provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (and, for users outside the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google LLC, 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA). AdSense uses cookies and similar technologies to show ads, limit how often you see an ad, measure performance and prevent fraud.

Third party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google’s Ads Settings, at www.aboutads.info or, in Europe, at www.youronlinechoices.eu. How Google uses information from sites that use its services is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent

Visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland see a consent message from Google’s certified consent management platform, which follows the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework (TCF). Until you decide, and if you decline, Google Consent Mode is set to “denied” for advertising and analytics storage. Personalized ads need your consent. Non-personalized ads also use cookies or identifiers for frequency capping, reporting and fraud prevention and therefore also need consent to store and access information on your device. Without that consent Google can only show limited ads, which do not use cookies or local storage for advertising and are not personalized; Google still processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver them and to prevent fraud. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 165(3) TKG 2021). You can change or withdraw your choice at any time via , which also appears in the footer of every page.

Google Analytics 4

We use Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC, measurement ID G-T00HRG2YVF) to understand which puzzles and guides are used. Before any Google script runs, analytics is disabled. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google Analytics is only loaded after you have consented in the consent message to storing and accessing information on a device (TCF purpose 1), to Google as a vendor and to analytics storage. Elsewhere it loads only if the consent message reports analytics as granted or not requiring consent, and reports that you have not opted out under a U.S. state privacy law; missing or unclear signals keep it off. When you withdraw consent via “Privacy settings”, analytics is switched off immediately. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. Data is kept for the retention period set in our account, at most 14 months. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Transfers to Google LLC in the USA rely on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Fonts and other resources

The fonts Fraunces and Atkinson Hyperlegible are served from this website. Apart from the Google advertising and analytics scripts described above, no scripts, fonts or styles are loaded from other servers.

Email

If you write to us, we use your address and message only to answer and delete them when they are no longer needed, unless legal retention periods apply (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. As we do not store personal data about visitors ourselves, most requests concern Google, whose controls are available at myaccount.google.com.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Austria this is the Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, dsb@dsb.gv.at, www.dsb.gv.at; you can also contact the authority in your country of residence.

Notice for U.S. residents

Under some U.S. state privacy laws, such as the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, the use of advertising cookies for cross-context behavioral advertising can count as “selling” or “sharing” personal information. Where these laws apply, Google’s consent message offers an opt-out, which you can reopen via “Privacy settings”. We do not sell personal information in any other way and do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.
